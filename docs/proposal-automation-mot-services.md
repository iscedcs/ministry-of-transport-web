PROPOSAL FOR AUTOMATION OF MINISTRY OF TRANSPORT SERVICES
ISCE Digital Concept is technical outfit with focus on IT, process automation, management audit and revenue aggregation all levels. Our range of services are designed to improve the organizational and management approaches of our clients’ businesses and strengthen performances while boosting efficiency. These services include developing unique solutions driven by ICT to optimize government processes and aggregate revenue. Leveraging on our deployment of Transpay electronic daily toll system for Anambra state government, we are very optimistic we can make a significant difference in administration and managing the Ministry Administration services.
WHY AUTOMATE?
Process Automation is to ensure that services rendered by the State Ministry of Transport Services are integrated into one single platform through the usage web application and ICT gargets. The integrated application is to enable multiple processes to happen in a seamless and effortless manner thus enhancing transparency, accountability and efficiency.
The Services to automate are but not limited to Accident Vehicle Inspection Report, Park Management, Mass Transit registration and renewal, Private Cab registration, Logistics services etc.
Mode of Operation
We wish to work with the Ministry of Transport in providing an automated platform which will incorporate all the services provided by the Ministry for efficiency and effective service delivery, while the Ministry will provide policy direction, monitoring, and evaluation of the automation. It is important to note at this point that the platform and associated systems shall be deployed and maintained at no cost to the Ministry. The operation will run on a PPP system, with a sharing formula to be agreed later.
CONCLUSION
Business process automation is current global trend, and it has become paramount that organization and institution must automate their services for effective and efficient management. The benefits and advantages of an automated system cannot be over emphasized, it will not only increase revenue but also enhance the skills and professionalism of the Ministry personnel.
We shall be hopeful that our proposal is favorably considered and we will be officially invited for discussion on the issues raised herein. We look forward to your gracious consideration.
