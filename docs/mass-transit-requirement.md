MINISTRY OF TRANSPORT
STANDARDS FOR REGISTRATION/APPROVAL OF MASS TRANSIT COMPANY/FLEET OPERATORS IN THE STATE
Preamble
The Ministry in line with its mandate and Anambra State Road Traffic Law hereby make the following Policy Guidelines on Registration of Mass Transit Companies/Fleet Operators in Anambra State as follows:
The mass transit company/fleet operator is to apply to the Ministry of Transport for registration/approval to operate in the State.
The applying company shall have minimum of five (5) branded vehicles in its fleet to be considered as a mass transit/fleet operator.
The company shall provide the following information/documents to the Ministry:
Certificate of Incorporation from corporate affairs commission.
Director’s tax clearance certificate
Director’s passport photograph.
Company’s ASIN Number.
Location/address of Head Office and branch offices in the State.
Business Premises Certificate.
ANSAA Registration Certificate/Permit.
Proposed major terminal/operating base and other terminals/depots in the State. This may be owned or leased (rented) by the company. Necessary documents to this effect such as lease/tenancy agreement, payment receipts, etc are to be provided.
List of Vehicles, types and models, their registration numbers, names of drivers, telephone numbers, routes being plied/to be plied, Inspection Reports on each vehicle, etc.
List of its staff, positions, telephone numbers and locations.
Sample (picture) of proposed colour (branding) of the mass transit vehicles.
Upon receipt of the above documents, the Ministry’s Parks Inspection and Revalidation Team will inspect the location(s) proposed to be used as operating terminals/depots for compliance with Ministry’s approved guidelines on private motor parks/loading bays. This is to be done within five (5) working days upon receipt of the application and endorsement by the Honourable Commissioner/Permanent Secretary. This applies to mass transit companies having their own private park/loading bay or intending to operate from existing public/private motor parks/loading bay.
The submitted documents/information will be verified by the Ministry.
The applicant shall pay a one-off application fee as prescribed by the Honourable Commissioner into the designated State Government paydirect Account using the Company’s Anambra State Identification Number (ASIN).
The vehicles in the fleet are to undergo mandatory roadworthiness inspection at any computerized vehicle inspection centres in the State and obtain Inspection Report to be submitted to the Ministry.
All mass transit vehicles are to possess valid/prescribed number plate (red on white background), motor vehicle documents – vehicle license, hackney license/stage carriage permit, certificate of roadworthiness and certificate of insurance (minimum of third party).
The drivers of such vehicles must possess valid national driver’s license and Ministry’s annual proficiency card.
Addition to or removal of any vehicle from fleet size submitted to the Ministry must be reported to the Ministry for update.
All the vehicles in the mass transit/fleet operator must bear one colour (brand) which must be approved by the Ministry.
The vehicles in the mass transit company are to be issued with unique numbers/QR codes for easy identification and monitoring by the Ministry.
Companies that satisfy the above requirements shall be issued with Permit to Operate Certificate which shall be renewed annually.
The Ministry shall specify monthly payments to be made to the State Government which shall be subject to review and applicable revenue codes.
Companies that meet above specified minimum criteria shall be approved to operate in the State through Approval Letter/”Certificate to Operate” issued to them by the Ministry.
