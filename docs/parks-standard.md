30th September, 2021

STANDARDS FOR APPROVING MOTOR PARKS IN THE STATE
Inspection of the Proposed Site
Requirements
Availability of sizeable land that can accommodate the vehicle traffic intended for the park and free from all encumbrances (cases/litigations).
Proposed site for the park should be able to accommodate all vehicles intended to use the park without overflow into/obstruction of the proximate public road.
Inspection of proposed site by the Ministry’s relevant Department/Team comprising HOD, Parks, HOD, VIS; HOD, T.Ops. and HOD, PRS.
Requirements/Standards for Park Approval
The park shall have adequate parking arrangement and order for vehicles waiting for their turns to load.
There should be distinct entrance into the park and exit out of the park with dwarf gate and gate house for checks.
The land should be paved, marked and have perimeter fence (minimum of wire mesh). If wall is used, must be dwarf fence with iron fence rail on top of the dwarf wall.
There should be conveniences – urinary and toilets (distinct) for males and females which must be kept clean and disinfected regularly. Mobile toilets can also be used.
There should be bore-hole or dedicated water tank for the park.
There should be refreshment shop/restaurant in the park but not shops selling alcoholic drinks or hawkers.
The park must have a ticketing office, waiting lounge (area) with seats for passengers close to the boarding section and Manager’s office.
There must be minimum of two (2) 9kg DCP fire extinguisher in the park.
Application and Approval Processes
Application letter from intending private Park Owner.
The application letter shall be accompanied with/detail the following:
Business/Transport Company’s Name;
Location of the motor park;
Evidence of CAC Registration;
Company ANSSID No;
Land ownership/lease agreement documents;
Name and telephone contact of the park manager;
The proposed park site is then inspected by the Team or designated personnel.
The Honourable Commissioner/Permanent Secretary issues “permit to build” to the company/applicant stipulating the requirements/standards for establishing private motor park in the State.
Upon completion of the park building and facilities by the applicant/demonstration of readiness to commence operations in the park, Ministry re-inspects the park to ensure the prescribed standards/criteria are complied with.
The re-inspection shall determine if the park meets the standards/criteria for approving private parks including:
Proximity to Public park;
Proximity to major transport route/public road;
Proximity to major road intersections;
The park is then evaluated for monthly/annual Motor Park Fee/Levy.
A confirmatory visit/inspection of the park by the Honourable Commissioner for Transport/Permanent Secretary is done if need be.
The Ministry then approves the private motor park notifying the park owner of terms and conditions that will govern the operations of the park including annual revalidation of the parks, reassessment for motor park fee, etc.
The Ministry reserves the right to revoke/withdraw its approval if the terms and conditions governing the park operations are not complied with or the applicant wilfully misinformed/misled the Ministry during the process of application and approval.
